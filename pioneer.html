The MWCS Origin Story

Sir Eliud Macharia Wanjiru is a distinguished Pedagogist, Educationist, Poet, Scriptwriter, Author, Drama Director, and Music Festival and Debate Trainer with vast expertise in the aforementioned fields. With many years of experience, he has guided learners to both national and international competitions, where they have earned numerous accolades and distinctions.

Long before the bright lights of the Kenya National Music Festival and Kenya National Drama and Film Festival stages, Sir Eliud walked the corridors of Kenyan schools witnessing a recurring challenge. Brilliant young performers with raw talent but lacking the professional guidance needed to shine on the national stage.

As a young educator and trainer, Sir Eliud saw students with incredible potential fall short simply because they lacked structured training, quality scripts, and professional directing. The festival circuit was unforgiving to those without proper mentorship.

This spark of indignation became the foundation for MWCS (Musical World Creative Society) and TWCS (Theatrical World Creative Society). Sir Eliud didn't just want to teach students how to perform; he wanted to give them the tools to win.

"Top notch creativity levels — every school has a champion inside. They just need someone to unlock it."

Today, under Sir Eliud's leadership, MWCS and TWCS have transformed over 45 schools across Kenya, producing 12 national award winners and maintaining a 97% advancement rate to the National Music Festival and National Drama and Film Festival.

Sir Eliud Macharia Wanjiru has taken the heavy silence of untapped potential and orchestrated it into a roaring chorus of confident, award-winning performers. The festival stage may be competitive, but with MWCS, every school has a voice.
